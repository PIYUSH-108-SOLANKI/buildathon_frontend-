# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Site Reliability Engineers (SREs), DevOps architects, platform leads, and infrastructure operators exploring microservice topology, investigating incidents, and overseeing autonomous AI remediation.

## Product Purpose
Autonomous AI Site Reliability Engineering control room that presents the entire microservice and AI recovery architecture as an interactive, luminous 3D physical system. Users click to zoom into physical service layers, inspect real-time streaming logs in a light terminal, observe Gemini-powered root cause analysis, and verify counterfactual recovery actions.

## Positioning
An interactive 3D architectural exploration of an autonomous AI reliability system, completely departing from conventional dark-mode terminal dashboards. It visualizes the full 5-layer SRE stack (Monitored Services, Observability, AI Reasoning, Autonomous Response, and Verification) in physical space with light studio aesthetics, peach and ivory warmth, and spatial camera focus.

## Operating Context
High-visibility engineering reviews, war-room architectural explorations, hackathon demonstration workstations. Clean, distraction-free daylight readability with smooth 3D interactions.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- 5 Interconnected 3D Architectural Layers:
  - Layer 1: Monitored Application (API Gateway, Orders, Payments, Storefront DB)
  - Layer 2: Observability Engine (Synthetic Prober, Metrics Collector, Log Collector, Incident Detector)
  - Layer 3: AI Intelligence (Gemini Brain, Culprit Finder, Counterfactual Simulator)
  - Layer 4: Autonomous Response (Safety Policy Gate, Docker Hands Executor, Circuit Breaker)
  - Layer 5: Verification & Reporting (Health Verifier, SLO Scorecard, Postmortem Engine)
- True 3D camera fly-to-zoom transitions on click.
- Continuous streaming logs in a readable light-themed terminal with auto-scroll and pause controls.
- Contextual inspection drawer displaying real-time metrics, AI diagnoses, and counterfactual evaluations.
- Strict isolation from backend codebase (backend remains strictly read-only).

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Autonomous AI SRE Platform (AIA-02)
- Visual Direction: Luminous 3D Architectural Visualization
- Color Palette: Peach (`#F9DDCA`), Ivory (`#FBFAF8`), Accent Orange (`#F28C55`), Charcoal (`#292623`)
- Tone: Elegant, precise, architectural, intelligent

## Product Principles
1. Architecture First: The system is an interconnected 3D entity, not a flat collection of charts.
2. Spatial Focus: Clicking an element flies the camera to that component while keeping the full system visible.
3. Luminous Clarity: Soft lighting and warm ivory backgrounds replace harsh dark terminals.
4. Uncompromising Truth: Clearly label live operational telemetry versus simulated drill data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast charcoal text on ivory surfaces (exceeding WCAG AAA).
- Multi-modal status indicators (shape, color, icon, and text).
- `prefers-reduced-motion` camera dampening.
